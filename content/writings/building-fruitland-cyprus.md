Fruitland Cyprus is a marketing website I built for a citrus farm
business: a hero carousel, product showcases, and a content pipeline
that lets non-technical owners edit everything themselves.

## The stack and why

I used **Next.js 15** for the framework, **Tailwind CSS** for styling,
and **Sanity CMS** for content. The reasoning was simple: the site had
to be fast, and the owners had to be able to change text and images
without calling a developer every time.
